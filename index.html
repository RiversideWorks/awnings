What we make

01

Traditional Awnings

Authentic heritage styling hand-crafted for high-street retail, boutiques, and hospitality venues.

Combine classic timber or robust aluminium framing with traditional canvas or solution-dyed acrylic covers. Designed specifically for conservation areas and listed buildings, each system can be finished with bespoke, oil-based hand-painted signwriting or high-precision corporate branding.

02

Retractable & Folding Arm Awnings

High-performance full-cassette and semi-cassette awnings for private gardens, restaurant patios and commercial building spaces.

Engineered and wind-tested, these systems feature fully enclosed cassettes that protect the fabric and folding arms from winter weather. Fully customisable with integrated dimmable LED lighting strips, radiant infrared heaters, and automated wind-vibration safety sensors.

03

Canopies & Pergolas

Fixed entrance structures, walkway covers and louvred roof systems for all-weather outdoor environments.

Robust, weather-resistant structures designed to turn exposed outdoor areas into usable year-round space. Available with motorised rotating aluminium louvres, fixed glass roofs, and integrated side drop-screens. Powder-coated in over 200 marine-grade RAL finishes with concealed internal rainwater drainage.

04

Recovering & Bespoke Engineering

Specialist bracketry, complete fabric recovering and expert signwriting services.

We extend the life of existing awnings and canopies by patterning and fitting new covers onto sound frameworks—whether built by us or another manufacturer. Our workshop also fabricates custom steel spreader plates and brackets for complex brickwork or rendered facades, alongside in-house signwriting and vinyl branding.

Explore

A lasting installation is decided long before anything turns up on site. It comes down to reading the building properly, spotting structural snags early, and getting the unglamorous engineering right — bespoke spreader plates, substrate load limits, BS EN 13561 wind ratings, and how every joint and fabric seam will behave after ten British winters.

Our team has spent their careers working across exactly these details, building a thorough grasp of what it takes to see a commercial or residential awning, blind, or canopy project through from first survey to final handover — including solar heat control requirements on modern glazed extensions where needed.

Our experience covers the entire project journey under one roof: understanding how a space actually needs to work, specifying a solution that fits the elevation, hand-crafting it accurately in our British workshop, coordinating the fit-out, and solving whatever the site throws at us on the day. We look at every project from every angle — not just how it looks on handover day, but how it will be built, operated, and maintained for years after we have left site.

Morrison Awnings is built around seasoned specialists — our operational lead, our workshop fabricators, and our in-house installation engineers, all of whom have spent years working with traditional Victorian awnings, folding arm systems, external blinds, and custom architectural canopies. That is where our eye for detail comes from: tight UK fabrication tolerances, complex masonry and steel fixings, integrated electrical controls, and the hand-finished quality that separates an enduring installation from an average one.
